To WebZen, and to MU Online fans across the years: This is a love letter, not a business. MU Online was part of our childhood: the first steps out of Lorencia, the nights spent hunting in Dungeon and Devias, the friends we made and the hours we lost together in a world that felt like our own. For many of us, those memories never faded, and this game was made out of that love. Every character, item, monster, map, sound and name in it belongs to WebZen Inc., who created MU and gave a whole generation those memories. This is not our game; it's a tribute to theirs.
