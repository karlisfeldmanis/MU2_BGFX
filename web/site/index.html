To WebZen, and to MU Online fans across the years: This is a love letter, not a business. MU Online was part of our childhood: the first steps out of Lorencia, the nights spent hunting in Dungeon and Devias, the friends we made and the hours we lost together in a world that felt like our own. For many of us, those memories never faded, and this game was made out of that love.
