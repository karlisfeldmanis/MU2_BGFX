Gameplay

  • Classic at heart

    The worlds, monsters, items and drops you remember, from Lorencia to Icarus.

  • Reworked spells

    Every class’s skills redone, with new effects, auras and animations, and rebalanced scaling.

  • Raids

    Raid the Golden Dragon, Blood Castle and Devil Square, or just level together.

  • Reworked Chaos Machine

    Refine gear, craft wings and combine jewels in a machine rebuilt from the ground up.

  • Runes

    Sockets on your gear for class runes and the rare Rune of Creation.

  • Quests

    From Lorencia’s townsfolk to your second class change.

  • Voiced NPCs

    Quest-givers who speak their lines, not just walls of text.

  • Modern controls

    Skills on Q, W, E and R with cooldowns, quick-key potions and auto-drink.

Look and sound

  • Sharper textures

    MU’s own artwork scaled up to today’s screens, crisp up close.

  • PBR materials

    Metal shines, leather is dull, stone is rough, all under the same light.

  • HDR lighting

    Sun, torches, lamps and bonfires, with film-like tonemapping.

  • Real-time shadows

    Trees, buildings, monsters and players all cast moving shadows.

  • Dynamic weather

    Rain that comes and goes, and storms with thunder and lightning.

  • Living world

    Trees and grass sway in the wind, and birds cross the sky.

  • 3D sound

    New sounds placed around you: the fight beside you, the monster behind you.

  • New interface

    Clean new windows for your bag, character, skills and trading.

To WebZen, and to MU Online fans across the years: This is a love letter, not a business. MU Online was part of our childhood: the first steps out of Lorencia, the nights spent hunting in Dungeon and Devias, the friends we made and the hours we lost together in a world that felt like our own. For many of us, those memories never faded, and this game was made out of that love. Every character, item, monster, map, sound and name in it belongs to WebZen Inc., who created MU and gave a whole generation those memories. This is not our game; it's a tribute to theirs.
